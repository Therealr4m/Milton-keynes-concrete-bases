Planning The Base Around The Furzton Property

The installation is planned around the property rather than assuming every garden can be accessed or prepared in the same way. This is particularly important on residential jobs where the route to the garden is narrow or existing landscaping needs to be worked around.

Narrow Side Access

Some Furzton gardens have limited side access through narrow pathways. Before work begins, we consider how excavation spoil will be removed and how the materials needed for the base will reach the working area. This helps us organise the job properly before the concrete is ordered and placed.

Existing Garden Layouts

Established family gardens can have paving, fencing, planting and changes in ground level that affect the working area. We take these into account when setting out the base so the installation works with the property rather than creating unnecessary disruption.

Ground Preparation

Where the existing ground is soft, the preparation beneath the concrete becomes particularly important. The ground needs to be dealt with before the concrete is placed so the finished base is not simply sitting over unsuitable material.

A Concrete Base Built Around The Actual Site

A Furzton garden can look straightforward from a distance, but the practical route into the working area can make a difference to how the installation is organised.

We assess the available access, the proposed footprint and the existing ground before setting out the work. This gives us a clearer idea of what needs to be removed, where materials need to go and how the concrete placement can be managed.

The aim is to leave a working base that is properly prepared for the intended building. The concrete itself is only one part of the installation. The preparation underneath it and the way the work is organised around the property matter just as much.

Why Proper Preparation Matters On Furzton Concrete Bases

A concrete base should be prepared for the building that will sit on it, not simply installed as a flat area of concrete in the garden.

If unsuitable or soft ground is left underneath the installation, movement can develop later. That can affect the base and potentially create problems for the structure above it. Dealing with the ground and preparation at the start is far more practical than trying to correct movement after the building has been installed.

Our approach is to look at what the property actually gives us to work with. On Furzton residential jobs, that can mean planning around narrow side access, working within an established garden and making sure the sub-base preparation suits the ground encountered during excavation.

Our Furzton Concrete Base Installation Process

We keep the installation process straightforward. The exact work depends on the size of the proposed base, the ground underneath it and the access available at the property.

Initial Site Assessment

We start by discussing what you are planning and looking at where the base needs to go. We consider the garden layout, the proposed dimensions and the route from the property to the working area.

Access And Work Planning

If the garden is reached through a narrow side pathway, this is considered before the installation is organised. The movement of excavation material, preparation materials and concrete needs to be planned around the available route.

Setting Out And Excavation

The proposed footprint is marked out and the area is excavated to remove unsuitable surface material and create the space required for the prepared base.

Sub-Base Preparation

Where required, MOT Type 1 is installed and compacted to create the prepared platform beneath the concrete. The amount of preparation depends on the ground encountered and the requirements of the project.

Formwork And Reinforcement

Formwork is set out to the required dimensions. Where reinforcement is appropriate for the project, it is positioned before the concrete is placed.

Concrete Placement And Finishing

The concrete is placed, levelled and finished across the prepared area. Once the installation is complete, the base is checked against the requirements of the project before the work area is left ready for the next stage.
