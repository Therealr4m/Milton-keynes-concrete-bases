Our Bletchley Concrete Base Installation Process

We keep the process straightforward and plan each installation around the property and the intended building. The exact work depends on the size of the base, the existing ground and the access available.

Initial Site Assessment

We discuss what you are planning, look at the proposed position of the base and consider how materials and equipment can reach the work area. Steps, raised sections and other access restrictions are identified at this stage.

Setting Out And Excavation

The installation area is marked out and excavated to remove unsuitable surface material and create the required space for the prepared base and concrete.

Sub-Base Preparation

A suitable MOT Type 1 sub-base is installed and compacted where required to create the prepared platform beneath the concrete. The amount of preparation depends on the existing ground and the demands of the structure.

Formwork And Reinforcement

Timber formwork is set out to the required dimensions. Where the project requires it, reinforcement is positioned before the concrete is placed.

Concrete Placement And Finishing

Fresh concrete is placed, levelled and finished to provide the surface required for the intended building. The work is then checked before the installation is completed.

The Installation Needs To Work With What You Are Building

The size and position of a concrete base need to be considered alongside the building that will sit on it. The available garden space, the proposed footprint and the ground beneath the installation all form part of the planning.
