Our Shenley Church End Concrete Base Installation Process

We keep the installation process straightforward, but the exact approach is planned around the property. A longer access route, an uneven garden or an existing surface can all affect how the work is organised before the concrete is placed.

Initial Project Discussion

We start by discussing what you are planning to build, the approximate dimensions and where you want the base positioned. We also ask about the route from the road to the garden because access can make a practical difference to the installation.

Site And Access Assessment

The proposed location is considered alongside the existing ground, surrounding garden layout and the available working route. This allows the excavation, material movement and installation to be planned before the work starts.

Excavation And Ground Preparation

The installation area is excavated to the required level, removing unsuitable surface material and creating space for the prepared foundation beneath the concrete slab.

Sub-Base Preparation

A suitable MOT Type 1 sub-base is installed and compacted where required to create a firm platform beneath the concrete. The amount of preparation depends on the existing ground and the demands of the structure being supported.

Formwork And Reinforcement

Formwork is set out to the required dimensions and checked before the concrete is placed. Where the project requires additional reinforcement, this is positioned as part of the preparation stage.

Concrete Placement And Finishing

Fresh concrete is placed, levelled and finished to provide the required surface for the intended building. The completed base is then checked before the working area is handed back ready for the next stage of the project.
