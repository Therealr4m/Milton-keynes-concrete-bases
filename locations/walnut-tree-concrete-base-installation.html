Why Ground Preparation Matters On Walnut Tree Concrete Bases

The finished concrete is only part of the installation. What sits underneath it matters just as much. If unsuitable ground or a poorly prepared sub-base is left underneath, movement can affect the finished base over time.

One issue we pay particular attention to is poor sub-base preparation. If the material beneath the concrete is not properly prepared and compacted, the base can be more vulnerable to movement or sinking.

That is why excavation and preparation are treated as part of the actual installation rather than something to rush through before the concrete arrives. The existing ground needs to be dealt with according to the conditions found on the property.

For homeowners arranging a concrete base in Walnut Tree, this is also where local practical experience matters. The work needs to fit the property, the garden and the access available on the day.

Our Walnut Tree Concrete Base Installation Process

We keep the installation process straightforward and organise the work around the property and the intended building. The exact preparation depends on the dimensions, existing ground and access available.

Initial Site Assessment

We discuss what you are planning, look at where the base is going and consider how the work area can be reached. Fences, steps, raised sections and other access restrictions are identified before the installation is planned.

Setting Out And Excavation

The proposed base is measured and marked out before excavation begins. Existing slabs, unsuitable surface material and any previous sections that need removing are dealt with as part of preparing the area.

Sub-Base Preparation

A suitable MOT Type 1 sub-base is installed and compacted where required. The preparation depends on the existing ground and the demands of the intended structure.

Formwork And Reinforcement

Formwork is positioned to the required dimensions and the installation is prepared for the concrete placement. Where reinforcement is appropriate for the project, it is positioned before the concrete is placed.

Concrete Placement And Finishing

The concrete is placed into the prepared area, levelled and finished to provide the surface required for the intended building. The installation is checked before the work area is completed.

The Installation Needs To Work With What You Are Building

The base needs to suit the footprint of the intended building as well as the available garden. We consider the proposed dimensions and position alongside the ground conditions and access before the installation starts.

Preparing The Base For The Next Stage

Whether the project involves a new outdoor building or replacing an existing area that is no longer suitable, the installation is organised around providing the foundation required for the next stage of the project.
