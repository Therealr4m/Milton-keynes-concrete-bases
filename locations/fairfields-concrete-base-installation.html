Proper Ground Preparation Matters On Fairfields Bases

The finished concrete is only as reliable as the preparation underneath it. If unsuitable ground or a poorly prepared sub-base is left beneath the installation, movement and sinking can become problems later.

We therefore deal with the existing ground before the concrete is placed. Where the ground is uneven or has previously been disturbed, the excavation and sub-base preparation are adjusted around the actual conditions found on site.

The aim is not to add unnecessary work. It is to make sure the base has been prepared for what will sit on it and for the conditions underneath it.
